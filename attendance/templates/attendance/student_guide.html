Student Quick Guide

Everything you need to track your attendance with SWASA
Quick Look
Website: swasa-attendance.onrender.com
Login with: Email address + your password
Password: the one you chose when you registered — not your Student ID
1
Set Up Your Account
Go to the registration page and fill in your Full Name, Student ID, Level, Email address, and a Password of your choice.
A 6-digit verification code is sent to the email you provided. Enter that code on the next page to activate your account.
Once verified, you are automatically logged in and taken straight to the course registration page — no extra steps needed.
Use any valid email address you have access to. Check your spam folder if you don't see the verification code within a minute.
2
Log In
On the login page enter your email address — the same one you registered with.
Enter the password you chose when you registered. This is not your Student ID — it is the password you set during sign-up.
If you forget your password, click Forgot Password on the login page. A reset code will be sent to your email.
Never share your password with anyone. Your Student ID is only used for identification — it is not your password.
3
Register Your Courses
Click Register Courses in the sidebar. You will see all courses available for your level and semester.
Check every course you are taking this semester, then click Register. You only need to do this once per semester.
Register early — the course list may be locked after the registration deadline.
4
Receive Your TA Code in Class
During each class session, your Teaching Assistant will generate a unique 2–5 digit code and hand it to you in person.
Each code is unique to you and this specific session. It cannot be shared or used more than once.
5
Submit Your Attendance Code
Go to your Dashboard. Find the course and locate the current week in the session list.
Click Submit Code, type the code your TA gave you, then tap Submit Attendance.
The week row will immediately update to a green "Submitted" badge — no page reload needed.
Submit as soon as your TA hands you the code — codes expire after the session ends.
6
Track Your Attendance Progress
Your Dashboard shows each course with an attendance progress bar and a score (e.g., 5 / 8).
Click My History in the sidebar for a full week-by-week breakdown with colour-coded status badges:
Attended — code submitted for this session
Absent — no code submitted
Upcoming — session has not happened yet
7
Export Records & Get Support
Click Export CSV in the sidebar to download your full attendance record as a spreadsheet for personal reference.
If you have a problem, click Support in the sidebar to submit a help ticket. The admin team responds within 24–48 hours.

Student Pro Tips
Save the website to your phone's home screen for quick access.
Submit your code right away — don't wait until after class.
Check your history weekly to catch any missed sessions early.
Use a strong, unique password — not something easy to guess. Never share it.
Export your CSV at the end of semester for your personal records.
