SWASA Attendance

Your Student Guide

A simple guide to tracking your attendance with the SWASA Attendance System
University of Ghana, Department of African and Asian Languages
📌 Quick Look
Website: swasa-attendance.onrender.com
Login: Use your Student ID for both your username and password
Example: If your ID is 10809707, use it for both fields.
1
Set Up Your Account
Head to the registration page and create your account using your student information.
📝
Registration Form — Fill your details
What you'll need: Your Student ID, UG Email (@st.ug.edu.gh), and academic level.
2
Log In
Simply use your Student ID for both your username and password to log in.
🔐
Login Page — Enter Student ID
Crucial: Your Student ID serves as both your username and password. Keep it confidential and never share it!
3
Enroll in Your Courses
Choose all the courses you're taking this semester.
📚
Course Selection — Check your courses
Helpful Tip: You only need to register once per semester. You can update your course selections anytime before the deadline.
4
Receive Your TA Code During Class
In every class session, your Teaching Assistant will provide a unique 2–5 digit code.
🎫
TA gives you a code during class
Please Note: Each code is unique to you and your specific class session. It cannot be shared with others.
5
Submit Your Attendance Code
From your dashboard, locate the current week, click "Submit Code," and enter the code provided by your TA.
⌨️
Dashboard — Enter code and submit
Great News! Once submitted, the status will update to "Submitted" with a green checkmark.
6
Monitor Your Progress
Check your attendance history to see which weeks you've attended or missed.
📊
History Page — See your attendance record
Present — Code submitted successfully
Absent — No code submitted
Upcoming — Session yet to occur
7
Download Your Records (Optional)
You have the option to download your attendance record as a CSV file for your personal reference.
📥
Export CSV — Download your attendance data
📞 Need a Hand?
If you need assistance, use the Support feature on your dashboard to submit a ticket. You can also reach out to your TA during class hours. Our admin team aims to respond to support tickets within 24–48 hours.

💡 Student Pro Tips
Save the website link to your phone's home screen for super quick access.
Your Student ID is your only password – keep it safe and secure!
Submit your code right after your TA gives it to you, so you don't forget.
Make it a habit to check your attendance history weekly to stay on top of your progress.
At the end of the semester, export your attendance as a CSV for your personal records.
