Help & Guide

How to use the attendance portal

A step-by-step guide to registering, checking in to class, and tracking your attendance.

At a glance

Sign in with your email address and password
Check in with the 6-digit code from your TA
Exam eligibility: don't miss 3 sessions in a row or 4 in total per course
Support responds within 24–48 hours
1 Set up your account
On the registration page, enter your full name, Student ID, level, email address, and a password of your choice.
A 6-digit verification code is sent to your email. Enter it on the next page to activate your account.
Once verified, you are signed in and taken to course registration.
Use an email address you can access. Check your spam folder if the code doesn't arrive within a minute.
2 Sign in
Enter the email address you registered with.
Enter the password you chose at sign-up — this is not your Student ID.
Forgotten it? Use Forgot password on the sign-in page and a reset code will be emailed to you.
3 Register your courses
Open Register Courses from the sidebar to see every course for your level and semester.
Select each course you are taking, then submit. You only do this once per semester.
4 Check in to class
During each session, your teaching assistant reads out a 6-digit code that is unique to you and that session.
On your Dashboard, find today's class and select Check In.
Enter the code and confirm. The session updates to Present immediately — no page reload needed.
Your code cannot be shared or reused. Entering another student's code is recorded as misuse.
5 Track your attendance
Your Dashboard shows overall attendance plus a progress bar and score for each course.
Open My History for a full week-by-week record:
Present code confirmed for the session
Absent no code confirmed
Not recorded session hasn't happened yet
6 Export records & get support
Use Export CSV in the sidebar to download your full attendance record as a spreadsheet.
Have a problem? Use Contact Support to raise a request. A teaching assistant responds within 24–48 hours.

Tips
Add the portal to your phone's home screen for quick access.
Check in during class — don't wait until you've left.
Review your history weekly to catch any missed sessions early.
Use a strong, unique password and never share it.
Export your records at the end of the semester for your own reference.
